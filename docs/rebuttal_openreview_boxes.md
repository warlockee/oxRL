**Q2 — Qwen3.** We cannot claim it without running it, and we deliberately do not. The mechanisms we identify (initialization dominance: 15.7→1.07pp spread compression at 1.5B; format-compliance regime at small scale) are not tied to Qwen 2.5 internals and replicate on Gemma 3. Because Qwen3's instruct initialization differs substantially, Finding 2 predicts the initialization effect will again dominate the algorithm effect — a falsifiable prediction we will add to the Discussion.

**Q3 — 5 vs 3 seeds.** Seed budget followed the statistical load: N=5 where headline significance tests live (7B core methods, p<0.001 after Bonferroni; the 20-variant 1.5B study = 100 runs), N=3 for supporting grids (1.5B core, IPO/KTO-7B, 14B, Gemma, controls), N=1 only for footnoted exploratory cells. Per-cell N is printed in every table; a sentence in §3 will state the policy.

**Q4 — Zero variance at 0.5B.** Base rows have no ± because ± is variance over *training* seeds and base models undergo no training. The σ=0 cells were real, separately-trained runs that produced bit-identical accuracies: in the framework version used for the March runs the data sampler received a fixed default seed, so runs differing only in `run.seed` were bit-reproducible (fixed in oxRL v1.9.0; the 1.5B/7B tables already use post-fix reruns). We re-ran the **entire 0.5B grid** under the fixed mechanism (Table-1 protocol): SP-RFT 33.59±0.26, DPO 33.18±0.31, IPO 33.97±0.68, KTO 33.38±0.98, SimPO 23.55±5.19. Variance is genuine and nonzero; original point estimates were accurate to <1pp; the exact SP-RFT=DPO coincidence disappears; SimPO's σ=5.19 independently replicates the paper's SimPO-instability finding at a third scale. The 0.5B column will be replaced with these numbers and the mechanism documented.
